Workflows — step configurators

a reference for every step type and its options — explore all the permutations here

This page is a catalog of every step type and its options — use it to explore the permutations of each, not a single workflow. The step name is the editable title at the top of each inspector (click it) — it’s the step’s label on the canvas and the prefix in data references (e.g. Triage ▸ priority). Anywhere you pick data, the dropdown is the same data-in-scope picker as the data-picker page — grouped by source, typed, searchable. The space to the right shows that step’s output or a note. The end-to-end example — and the live dry run — live in the step builder; the examples here are just illustrative.

A workflow can have several triggers. New watches a source for items as they arrive. Changed watches a chosen set of items and fires when one is updated. Or run on a schedule.
