Enable Goals in Settings → System → Experimental, then choose Goal from the chat composer's + menu or type `/goal` to assign a goal and follow its editable plan and progress. Add an objective after `/goal` to prefill the goal dialog. Agents can schedule their own returns, ask for missing information, and complete goals in the same conversation. Goal badges show when work is running, scheduled, or paused, with controls to pause, resume, or cancel. Goals starts off; turning it off pauses saved goals and keeps their progress.

Answer goal questions in the message composer, moving between questions without losing drafts. Send partial answers while the agent continues useful work, or switch to ordinary chat. The agent resolves answered questions and withdraws obsolete ones; earlier questions and answers remain in the conversation and goal history. Answering a paused goal keeps it paused.
