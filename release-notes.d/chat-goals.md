Enable Goals in Settings → System → Experimental to assign a goal to a chat and follow its editable plan and progress. Agents can schedule their own returns, ask for missing information, and complete goals in the same conversation. Goal badges show when work is running, scheduled, or paused, with controls to pause, resume, or cancel. Goals starts off; turning it off pauses saved goals and keeps their progress.
