Refreshed the visual design of the chat, sidebar, and composer: a friendlier
self-hosted Inter typeface throughout (replacing the monospace brand font),
softer rounded corners, and a lighter sidebar. The chat's empty-state heading
and cards are bigger and better balanced, the sidebar's active section is
easier to read, and the composer's send button is now an up arrow. The
composer's full-screen expand now grows to about 80% of the window's height
while keeping its normal width and floating over the conversation, instead of
taking over the whole panel.
